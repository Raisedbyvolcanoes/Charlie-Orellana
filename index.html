Welcome to the visual storytelling work of Charlie Orellana

Raised by Volcanoes is an independent film studio that collaborates with artists, organizations and brands for visual creative campaigns, from short documentaries, music videos, festival films to promotional videos, each project is inspired by the people, its culture and the relationship to the natural world.

Hello,

I’m Charlie Orellana, a creative director, filmmaker and photographer from Guatemala.

My work explores the relationship between people, places and nature, creating visual stories that connect culture, identity and the natural world. I collaborate with brands, organizations and artists who value thoughtful storytelling, cultural depth and a meaningful connection to the environments that inspire their work.

The essence of my work is influenced by my homeland origins, Guatemala’s nature, texture, culture, indigenous traditions and landscapes, especially the presence of volcanoes. They have taught me to observe with patience, create with intention, and approach filmmaking as a practice of preserving stories, places and traditions that matter.

Across documentaries, music films and commissioned campaigns, I create films and photography stories that honor cultural heritage, artistic expression and our relationship with the earth.
